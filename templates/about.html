About Plant Disease Classifier

AI-Powered Plant Care

Plant Disease Classifier is an advanced tool that uses artificial intelligence to help gardeners, farmers, and plant enthusiasts identify diseases in their plants.

Our system uses deep learning technology to recognize patterns in plant leaf images and identify diseases with high accuracy. By providing early detection, we help you take timely action to prevent crop loss and maintain plant health.

How It Works

1. Upload Image

Take a photo of the affected plant leaf and upload it to our system.

2. AI Analysis

Our AI model analyzes the image and identifies the disease.

3. Get Results

Receive the predicted disease along with relevant information about the plant condition.

Technology Behind the System

  • Deep Learning Model: EfficientNetB1 architecture trained on a large dataset of plant leaf images
  • Accuracy: 99.47% on test datasets
  • Disease Information: Provides information about the predicted plant disease and its condition
  • Backend: Flask with TensorFlow for real-time prediction
